Questions

Frequently asked

Is Instagram/Facebook/WhatsApp outreach compliant?+
Yes. 1pont uses official Meta Graph API and WhatsApp Business API — not browser scrapers or session hijacking. Instagram DM via the official Messenger Platform API follows Meta's 24-hour messaging window and opt-out rules. We build compliance guardrails (unsubscribe handling, rate limiting, suppression lists) into every channel by default.
Can I get my LinkedIn account banned using 1pont?+
1pont is built LinkedIn-optional — our focus is local business data, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and email. LinkedIn's automation enforcement is escalating (23% restriction rate within 90 days for automation users). We deliberately built around this risk rather than into it. You can still connect LinkedIn as an optional channel, but it's not our primary data or messaging source.
How is 1pont different from Phantombuster?+
Phantombuster is a scraping toolkit for technical users — it extracts data from social platforms using browser automation, which carries high ban risk on Instagram and Facebook. 1pont uses official APIs (no ban risk), includes email sequences, built-in contact verification, WhatsApp Business, and a CRM pipeline — all in one flat-fee platform. No coding required, no session scraping, no per-execution billing.
What's included in "built-in email verification"?+
Every contact enriched through 1pont is automatically verified for deliverability before entering any sequence. You never pay separately for NeverBounce, ZeroBounce, or similar tools. Our internal verification runs at the enrichment stage — not as an afterthought. This is the core reason Apollo exports bounce at 32–38% while 1pont-sourced contacts target under 3%.
When will 1pont launch?+
We're in private beta now with a small cohort of agencies and sales teams. The waitlist opens to early-access members first, with founder pricing locked for 12 months. Reply.io has announced competing Instagram/Facebook features — not yet shipped. Our goal is to be live and battle-tested before they do.
